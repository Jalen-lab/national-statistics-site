Japan by Numbers is an independent presentation of public statistics. Figures are rounded; every figure names its source and year on the Sources page.

Sources include the Statistics Bureau of Japan, the Cabinet Office, the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, the IMF World Economic Outlook (October 2025), the World Bank World Development Indicators (2024) and UN World Population Prospects (2024). No data is fetched from the network: the whole site runs from its own files.
